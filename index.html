A LETTER FROM HOSTEL

💌

Dear Sudhir Bhaiya, Monu Bhaiya, Ankit Bhaiya... and my little ones — Ansh, Khushi & Rudra ❤️

You know... this Raksha Bandhan feels a little different for me. Every year, when Rakhi comes around, there is a different kind of excitement in the house. The family getting together, all the laughter, sweets, Rakhi, photographs... and most importantly — spending that special time with all of you.

But this year, I am in the hostel. And honestly, no matter how much I try to act normal, deep down I am missing all of you so much. 🥺

The hardest part is knowing that this year I won't be able to tie Rakhi on your wrists with my own hands. Maybe for someone else, it might seem like a small thing... but not for me. Because Rakhi is not just a thread. It carries so many memories from our childhood and so much love in it. ❤️

Sudhir Bhaiya, I miss those little arguments with you, your occasional scolding, the care you show, and that special feeling of having an elder brother. I miss all of it. Maybe I don't always express it, but you are truly very important to me. ❤️

Monu Bhaiya, When we are at home, we don't always realize how precious the ordinary moments are. But when you are far away, those random conversations, silly jokes, little fights, and everyday family moments are the things you miss the most.

Ankit Bhaiya, I will miss you a lot this Rakhi too. Sometimes simply sitting together in the same room, laughing for no special reason, or annoying each other over the smallest things... those are actually some of the most special moments we have. ❤️

And my little ones — Ansh, Khushi and Rudra... I miss you all too. Your mischief, your smiles, your little conversations... Rakhi will feel a little incomplete without all of you. 🥹

❤️ This year, there may not be a Rakhi on my wrist, but the love I have for all of you in my heart is still the same... maybe even a little more. ❤️

On this Rakhi, when you all see each other, I want you to remember me too. Just imagine that somewhere in a hostel room, your sister is thinking about all of you. I may not be able to celebrate Rakhi with you in person, but my heart is right there with all of you.

And yes... when I finally come home, I expect a proper celebration for the Rakhi I missed. 😂❤️ Double the sweets, double the photographs, double the fun... and yes, my Rakhi will still be waiting!

Being away from home has taught me something important — sometimes you don't realize how precious your people are until you are far away from them. And all of you are some of the most precious people in my life.

So this Rakhi, even though I can't tie the Rakhi myself... please remember that my love, my wishes, and my heart are right there with you.

Happy Raksha Bandhan to my favourite people. ❤️

Missing you all a little extra today... — Your Sister, Shrishti 💗
